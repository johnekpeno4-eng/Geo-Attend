How to read these results

Passing scans are grouped as genuine samples. Scored failed scans are grouped as impostor samples, so collect those by having a different student deliberately try the selected account. A genuine finger can also fail because of blur, lighting, or placement; review the test conditions before changing the threshold. Scores are calibration measurements, not proof of identity.

No images or fingerprint templates are saved in the calibration CSV.
